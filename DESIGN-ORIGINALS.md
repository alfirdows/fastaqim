# Fastaqim: original design record

Everything below was designed and drawn for Fastaqim. It is not a template, a kit, or a copy of another app or website. The git history of this repository holds the dated commits that created each item. They are the record of authorship.

## Brand idea
- The name comes from "So be steadfast as you have been commanded" (Qur'an, Surah Hud 11:112).
- The sign is **the true dawn**: the straight, horizontal line of light at Fajr (Sahih Muslim 1094).
- The whole design is built on that one line.

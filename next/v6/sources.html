Islamic scholars' guidance used in simple words

  • al-Ghazali, Ihya' 'Ulum ad-Din: muhasabah, riyadat an-nafs (training the self little by little), breaking habits by their opposite.
  • Ibn al-Qayyim, Madarij as-Salikin and al-Fawa'id: guarding thoughts before they become actions, small steady deeds, tawakkul with taking the means.
  • Yasser al-Hazimi (Thmanyah podcast talks): practical self-discipline, starting small, honest self-review. Used as a reference for tone and practice only; nothing is quoted.
